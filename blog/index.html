When You Can't Measure What Matters: A Closed-Loop Harness for Probability-of-Default Estimation

Lending data only labels the loans a past policy approved — so the declined cohort, exactly where a new PD model must be right, is the one it can't be graded on. Plant the truth in a synthetic causal world, hide it the way approval policies do, escalate selection severity until correction fails, and report the operating frontier as a distribution, not a point.
