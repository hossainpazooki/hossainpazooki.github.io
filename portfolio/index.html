Portfolio — evaluation · execution · provenance

Three planes, one section. Nothing credited until its gate is proven able to fail.

Everything I build sits somewhere in this cut: evaluation for what is measured, execution for what may act, and provenance for the record both rest on. The same discipline the Carryover paper runs on — decision rules fixed before any run, verdicts recorded, outcomes reported as decided — is how these repositories are built.
