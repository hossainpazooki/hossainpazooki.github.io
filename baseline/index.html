the underpinning of vantage × parallax

The shift is cheap.
The baseline is the instrument.

VANTAGE is the viewpoint: it fixes where you stand when you read — as-of date D, nothing accepted after D. PARALLAX is the shift: what the same fact appears to do when the viewpoint changes. This page is the third term, the one the other two stand on — the baseline, the known, rigid separation between viewpoints. Optics settled this first: a shift becomes a measurement only across a baseline you can cite.

The bracket is honest about its own state: the first measurement across this baseline has been taken. Scoped cik % 10, 17,787 filer-quarters — as-of and restated YoY revenue growth come out bit-identical for 89.4% of them, 3.4% diverge by more than 10pp, 1.51% flip sign. Rare, but violent where it lands. Re-read 150 days later the divergence barely moves, and that is the part that counts: what shifted was the data, not the read path. Attribution is exactly what the separation buys, and it has now been paid out once. A full-surface run stays unclaimed; STATUS.md bounds the rest.

Turning the same distinction outward changed what it is evidence for. Read against a commercial market-data and SEC-fundamentals API — its published surface, not its data — the filings and fundamentals endpoints carry no as-of mode and no acceptance instant; the field dating a period is the most recent filing that touched it. The same vendor timestamps trade and quote receipt to the nanosecond. So the zero-baseline failure below is not a cautionary sketch — it is the default shape of the surface most consumers read, and the precision that would fix it already exists one product line over.

1838 · the first baseline that held

Friedrich Bessel picked 61 Cygni for its restless proper motion, took Earth's orbit as his ruler, and read the shift: 0.3136″ ± 0.0202″ — about 10.4 light-years, the first measured distance to a star. The value on record today, two centuries of instruments later: 0.286″ — 11.4 light-years. The first parallax ever taken carries its own as-filed → restated history, and it embarrasses nothing: the viewpoint was recorded beside the value, so the revision is data rather than an error. That bookkeeping — the viewpoint stored next to the fact — is exactly what VANTAGE's gold model is.

the argument

The pun is load-bearing — and it stands on its invisible third term.

Naming the two visible terms was the easy part: VANTAGE produces viewpoints, PARALLAX measures what shifts between them. But nothing about two viewpoints makes them comparable. What does is the separation between them being known before the measurement and rigid during it — in astronomy the surveyed orbit, here the contract. Every claim either repo makes routes through it.

Withhold the baseline and the shift is still visible — it just stops meaning anything. A consumer without the contract measures its own read path and files the artifact as a revision. That is why the consumer gate runs before any study does, and why an unevaluable read refuses instead of passing.

what the baseline is made of

A rangefinder is mostly tube. This baseline is mostly refusals.

A coincidence rangefinder is two windows at the ends of a rigid tube — the tube's known length is the instrument. The pair's tube has three sections, each load-bearing, each earned by a refusal rather than a promise.

strut 01ADR-0001

One surface

Published gold is the only thing PARALLAX may read — never bronze, never silver, never a path around the boundary. Sibling, not fork; the consumer never writes upstream.

  • one end of the baseline is welded to the boundary: no read below the published surface
  • the producer's guarantee is scoped to the gold model — deliberately not end-to-end through the ingest seam, which is why an independent consumer gate exists at all
strut 02Reader protocol

One protocol

Gate checks and study queries are written against a Reader protocol — never a path, never a connection string — so every surface answers the same question with the same semantics.

  • naive datetimes rejected at the protocol boundary; a zoneless timestamp moves the PIT boundary between machines
  • the reader refuses to glob a Delta table — the bare-glob double-count after an overwrite is a pinned test, not a war story
strut 03the twin rule

Proof it can fail

A gate that has never been red holds nothing up. Every surface earns two cells: green on the live gold, red on a planted twin staged the same way.

  • live: three checks over 440,661 as-of rows — zero violations (2026-08-07, replayable)
  • twin: the same subset, one row mutated — caught as exactly one violation per affected check; the good copy of the staging gated PASS first
  • fixture twins for the other two mutation families run red in CI on every push

Lane 1 (local Parquet) holds both cells today. Lanes 2–3 — Unity Catalog, then Snowflake over Iceberg — are empty on purpose: the sentence isn't claimable there yet, and the page that bounds those claims is STATUS.md, not this one. Each lane re-earns both cells against a twin staged in that catalog; vendor read paths differ, the invariants don't get to.

how baselines fail

Zero, unknown, flexing — three failures, three different lost claims.

Each strut exists because its failure mode is real. Every optics failure has a system twin — and each twin has either been caught in this build already or is pinned so it stays caught.

00zero baseline
in the instrument

One viewpoint. Nothing shifts; a near star and the far background are indistinguishable. There is no measurement to get wrong.

in the pair

Latest-only reads — no as-of discipline, so lookahead has nowhere to show up and quietly becomes the dataset. A backtest built on it isn't wrong about the edge; it isn't about an edge at all.

01unknown baseline
in the instrument

The shift is right there on the plate, but with no citable separation it never becomes a distance — a number with no meaning.

in the pair

An unproven contract. A divergence between two reads can't be attributed: data revision, or read-path artifact? An always-green gate lives here too — green that has never been shown able to go red cites nothing.

02flexing baseline
in the instrument

The separation moves between the two observations and the instrument manufactures shift — false parallax, indistinguishable from signal on a single pair of plates.

in the pair

Read semantics drifting between reads: the Delta bare-glob double-count after an overwrite; zoneless SEC timestamps parsed in local zone, moving the PIT boundary between machines. Both real, both caught, both pinned as tests.

The struts and the failures are the same list with the sign flipped — which is exactly what it means for the baseline to be the instrument.
