Carryover: The Reuse Margin of a Stale KV Cache in Long-Horizon Agents first author · NeurIPS 2026 Workshop on Long-Context Foundation Models

Can the next step of an agent reuse the cache the last one left behind? At 35 recorded coding-agent handoffs, the median same-model removal fraction is zero across three models, yet reused states still shift next-token predictions. Cache agreement and prediction sensitivity are separate checks.

Know · Act · Measure

the three-plane architecture, in full →

Everything I build sits in one cut: a data plane for what is known, an authorization plane for what may act, and an audit boundary that holds both to their evidence. Nothing is credited until its gate is proven able to fail.

01 · know DATUM a reference frame for checkable claims: produced, adversarially re-derived, recorded as replayable verdicts 02 · act INTENT irreversible actions pass a deterministic gate; agents propose, the gate disposes; unevaluable never collapses into a pass 03 · measure RIGOR the boundary that audits both planes: a report of success is a claim, and no claim counts until its gate has been seen to fail
